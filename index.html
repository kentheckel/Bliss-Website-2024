Color Social

                

                
                
                

                    
Your username is literally a color.

                    
                    
So I built this app called Color Social. The idea is pretty simple - 
                    instead of picking a username like everyone else, you just... are a color. 
                    Like your username is a hex code. Mine is #FB7F25 (that's the orange you see).

                    
                    
I'm building 9 different apps, each one is its own color community:

                    
                    
    
                        
  •  Orange (out now!)
    • 
                        
  •  Red
    • 
                        
  •  Yellow
    • 
                        
  •  Green
    • 
                        
  •  Blue
    • 
                        
  •  Purple
    • 
                        
  •  Pink
    • 
                        
  •  White
    • 
                        
  •  Black
    • 
                    

                    
                    
Each app is totally separate - what you post in Orange stays in Orange. 
                    It's kind of like having 9 different social media worlds based on color.

                    
                    
ColorSocial Orange is live on the iOS App Store if you wanna check it out!

ChannelTrack

                    
channeltrack.xyz

                    
Automatically sync YouTube channel data into your Notion workspace

                

                
                
                

                    

                        
I've been asked a ton of times now how I track YouTube channels & put them into Notion. So I built this tool to let you do it yourself!

                    

                

                
                
                

                    
What is ChannelTrack?

                    
ChannelTrack automatically syncs YouTube channel data into your Notion workspace - daily stats, growth metrics, and viral video detection, all updated automatically.

                

                
                
                

                    
What it does:

                    
    
                        
  • Tracks subscriber counts, views, and uploads for any YouTube channel
    • 
                        
  • Syncs fresh data to Notion databases every day (no manual work)
    • 
                        
  • Detects viral videos and trending content in real-time
    • 
                        
  • Analyzes growth patterns and competitive benchmarks
    • 
                        
  • Organizes everything in your Notion workspace for easy team collaboration
    • 
                    

                

                
                
                

                    
Why use it:

                    
Perfect for content creators, media teams, and agencies who need to monitor competitors, spot trends early, and make data-driven decisions. Stop manually copying YouTube stats into spreadsheets - let ChannelTrack handle it automatically.

                

                
                
                

                    
Pricing:

                    

                        
Free plan available to try it out. Paid plans start at $9/month for tracking 5 channels with daily auto-sync.

                    

                

                
                
                

                    
Learn more at channeltrack.xyz

Uptides

                    
uptides.ai

                    
AI-powered trend detection for YouTube creators and media teams

                

                

                    

                        
I got tired of manually scrolling through Twitter and Reddit trying to figure out what's trending. So I built an AI that does it for me and tells me what to make videos about.

                    

                

                

                    
What is Uptides?

                    
Uptides uses AI to scan the internet for rising trends, viral moments, and breaking stories relevant to your niche — then tells you exactly what to create and when to post it.

                

                

                    
What it does:

                    
    
                        
  • Monitors social platforms, news, and search trends 24/7
    • 
                        
  • Identifies trending topics before they peak
    • 
                        
  • Generates video ideas, titles, and thumbnail concepts
    • 
                        
  • Sends real-time alerts when something in your niche is about to blow up
    • 
                        
  • Tracks competitor uploads and performance for strategic timing
    • 
                    

                

                

                    
Why use it:

                    
Stop guessing what to post. Uptides tells you what people are searching for right now, so you can ride the wave instead of chasing it. Built for creators who want to be first, not fifteenth.

                

                

                    
Status:

                    

                        
Currently in private beta. Built by the same team behind ChannelTrack. Sign up for early access at uptides.ai.

                    

                

                

                    
Learn more at uptides.ai

PodMonitor

                    
podmonitor.club

                    
The podcast leaderboard for YouTube

                

                

                    
What is PodMonitor?

                    
PodMonitor helps podcast teams follow the shows gaining attention on YouTube. Explore rankings based on views, discover breakout videos, and see how shows compare across genres.

                

                

                    
What it does:

                    
    
                        
  • Publishes weekly and monthly charts with rankings that stay fixed once published
    • 
                        
  • Tracks live leaderboards and trending videos as views grow
    • 
                        
  • Breaks down each show's full episodes, clips, and Shorts
    • 
                        
  • Filters podcast rankings by genre for relevant comparisons
    • 
                        
  • Offers guest research for signed-in users, including appearances and episode performance
    • 
                    

                

                

                    
Why use it:

                    
Keep up with competitors, spot rising shows, and research potential guests with the numbers in one place. Built for podcast creators, producers, and media teams by Antisocial Friends Club.
