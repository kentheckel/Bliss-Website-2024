Color Social

Your username is literally a color.

So I built this app called Color Social. The idea is pretty simple - instead of picking a username like everyone else, you just... are a color. Like your username is a hex code. Mine is #FB7F25 (that's the orange you see).

I'm building 9 different apps, each one is its own color community:

  • Orange (out now!)
  • Red
  • Yellow
  • Green
  • Blue
  • Purple
  • Pink
  • White
  • Black

Each app is totally separate - what you post in Orange stays in Orange. It's kind of like having 9 different social media worlds based on color.

ColorSocial Orange is live on the iOS App Store if you wanna check it out!
