Engagement on our losses (7.60%) beats the old regime's wins (6.90%). Wins can't do that.

THE OUTPUT

2.5× the posts (8,514 → 21,602) while engagement rate rose 6.63% → 7.80%.

THE SIGNATURE

Biggest gains hit the weakest platforms first: TikTok #24→#1 · IG #18→#1. That's an intervention, not a wins lift.
