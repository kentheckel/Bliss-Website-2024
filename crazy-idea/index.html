Episode treatment / How we make the filmThe concept

So, here’s the concept.

  1. 01

    I come to you.

    You show me around. I meet the team, learn how your business works, and see what you’re already doing on YouTube and social.

  2. 02

    Back at my studio…

    I dig into your niche, pin up titles and thumbnails, map how the accounts fit together, and build a revenue plan. Then I call brands on our roster.

  3. 03

    We workshop it in person.

    From the CEO to the interns, your creatives and on-camera talent try out the strategy. We make titles and thumbnails together so everyone knows their part.

  4. 04

    We make the first video.

    We help you publish, then follow the channel for a few months to see who comes back, what earns revenue, and what to make next.
