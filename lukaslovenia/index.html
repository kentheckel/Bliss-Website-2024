05Method & caveats

Read this before it goes in a deck.

readme.txt–▢✕
  • Six Apify runs on apify/instagram-hashtag-scraper, apify/instagram-scraper (×2), clockworks/tiktok-scraper, clockworks/tiktok-hashtag-scraper and apidojo/tweet-scraper · hashtags, keyword searches, and ~30 official, media and fan accounts.
  • 3,037 raw rows → 2,517 unique posts after de-duplicating on post ID (keeping the highest-engagement capture), then 1,546 inside the date window, then 425 whose own caption names a Lakers player or the team and places it in Slovenia. Matching only the hashtag we searched is not enough.
  • Engagement = likes + comments + shares/retweets + saves/bookmarks. Not comparable across platforms: X counts retweets, Instagram exposes no share count, TikTok counts saves.
  • Views are incomplete and not like-for-like. 47 of 59 Instagram posts return no play count at all, so the IG total is a floor. X reports impressions, which inflate against TikTok plays · X leading on “views” is a counting artifact, not a reach win.
  • Known gaps. TikTok’s hashtag actor completed 3 of 8 requested tags. 46 tier-A posts show zero engagement (mostly low-reach X replies) and pad the counts. Instagram Stories, YouTube Shorts, Reddit, Slovenian broadcast clips and Slovenian-language posts without a Latin-script keyword match are not covered at all.
  • Counts are a snapshot from 2026-08-24 17:23 UTC and will drift upward. Tier assignment is keyword-based and worth spot-checking before anything client-facing.
