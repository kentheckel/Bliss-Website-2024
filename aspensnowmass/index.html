07How it pays · three ways the content makes money, one thing it always sells

Lift tickets first. Then the partners pay for the content that sells them.

ALWAYS ON
The ticket funnel
Every video ends the same way: a tracked link to lift tickets and passes, in the description, the pinned comment, the end screen. We measure clicks and codes, not just views.
Video→Tracked link→Ticket page→Code at checkout
THE KPI · A THOUSAND VIEWS THAT SELL 800 TICKETS BEATS A MILLION THAT SELL NONE
THE BIG ONE
Partner integrations
Hublot, Audi, LVMH, X Games, Snow League: already your partners, now inside the videos. Audi drives the host to the mountain. Hublot times the challenge run. The gondola DJ wears the headphones.
FixedA flat rate per integration. Simple, predictable.
CPMThe brand bets on us. They pay per thousand views the video actually gets.
HybridA lower flat fee plus a CPM kicker once the video clears the number.
WHAT ONE INTEGRATION IS WORTH
500K views × $30–50 CPM
= $15K to $25K
PER VIDEO · ILLUSTRATIVE
SAME THREE STRUCTURES APPLY TO OUR ENGAGEMENT: FIXED, CPM, OR HYBRID · ON THE CALL
THE BONUS
AdSense
YouTube pays per thousand monetized views. You already qualify (1,000 subs, 4,000 watch hours). Long-form pays 20 to 50× what Shorts pay, so the host lane is what makes this line real.
