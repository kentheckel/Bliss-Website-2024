The Late Run — YouTube Strategy

Launching Chad Ochocinco's show from absolute zero — January 2026

Overview

We got brought in to launch The Late Run with Chad Ochocinco from the genesis. This channel started at zero — no subscribers, no views, no history. Launching any YouTube channel from zero is a huge bummer and a very unfun thing to try and do. So we didn't launch cold.

The Pillow Strategy

For two weeks leading up to launch we warmed the channel up: a trailer first, then clips and vertical videos. By the time the first few episodes dropped, the channel was primed — and launch weekend was a massive success.

What We Did

  • Full supplementary pipeline — every episode landed in our lap, and we made all the clips and vertical content for every platform.
  • Built toward a moment — the goal was to have the show fully optimized by the World Cup, about five months after launch, to attract sponsors. That's exactly what happened.
  • The ASFC podcast system — weekly episodes, horizontal clips, title & thumbnail testing, and outsized vertical output from day one.

Results

0 → 43.9K subscribers and 43.4M views in roughly 7 months

10M+ views a month through the growth phase — and 18M views in the last 28 days as the World Cup window hit, with nearly 12K subscribers added in that stretch alone. Optimized, sponsor-ready, and still climbing.
