All The Smoke Fight — YouTube Strategy

Rebranding the old Showtime Boxing channel into a boxing podcast powerhouse

Overview

All The Smoke Fight used to be the old Showtime Boxing YouTube channel, and a major focus for us was rebranding it toward a podcast. Originally it was just The Art of Ward podcast living here. Then we did an episode with Roy Jones Jr. that performed really well — so we built out different properties underneath the same channel.

What We Did

  • Guest-first booking — we achieved better results when we focused in on who our guests were going to be.
  • Built around the fight calendar — we interjected our story into the stories happening in mainstream boxing, which turned the show into a mainstream interview that fighters wanted to participate in.
  • Credibility through Roy Jones Jr. — his involvement made it an extremely valid boxing show that speaks directly to a very educated boxing audience.
  • Reactive properties — we built out more shows reacting to old fights, upcoming fights, and boxing news.
  • The ASFC podcast system — our standard Antisocial Friends Club strategy: weekly flagship episodes, high-impact horizontal clips, title & thumbnail testing on every upload, and outsized vertical output across every platform.

The wins came from long-form interviews paired with weekly short-form breaking-news moments — a system built to grow the show.

Results

1.93M subscribers · 61.6M views since we teamed up (Mar 2025)

Monthly views nearly tripled since we started — from ~2.1M to ~5.9M per 28 days — with upload output doubled.
