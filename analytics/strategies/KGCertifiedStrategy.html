KG Certified — YouTube Strategy

Giving Kevin Garnett's show its own home — and building a view machine around it

Overview

We came into KG Certified when it was still living on the main All The Smoke channel. We moved it over to its own unique channel — formerly its clips channel — and built that into the main home for the KG Certified podcast.

What We Did

  • A seasonal show, on purpose — KG Certified isn't always-on. They take summers off, which we think is a great thing: it gives the rest of the creator ecosystem room, and lets them come back in sharp.
  • The right voices — they talk basketball news, so we brought in the guests that fit the conversation.
  • Topics built for the duo — we found the subjects that made the most sense for them to riff on, then let them go wild and talk about whatever they wanted.
  • Capture everything — our foundation was built to catch that content and squeeze as many views out of every moment as possible, running the standard ASFC podcast strategy: flagship episodes, horizontal clips, title & thumbnail testing, and heavy vertical output on every platform.

Results

184K → 260K subscribers (+41%) · 39.9M views since we teamed up (Mar 2025)

A clips channel turned into the show's true home.
