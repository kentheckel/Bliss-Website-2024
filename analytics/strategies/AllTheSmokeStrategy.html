All The Smoke — YouTube Strategy

Strategy consultant · Matt Barnes & Stephen Jackson's flagship show

Overview

I joined All The Smoke as a strategy consultant to support the ongoing uplift of their shows. Early on, we consolidated their channels and built out a high-output, data-driven strategy centered on the main show.

The Framework

We built a repeatable system so the team could support:

  • Weekly podcast releases — a consistent flagship cadence the audience can count on.
  • High-impact horizontal clips — pulling the strongest moments from every episode.
  • A title & thumbnail testing program — run around every upload to make each one as high-impact as possible.
  • A robust vertical output — every social platform gets fed an outsized amount of content compared to competitors, driving high view counts for the channel off of new platform features.

Results

1.33M subscribers · 717M+ total views · 3,800+ uploads

Averaging 15M+ views per month over the last year, with peak months topping 21M.
