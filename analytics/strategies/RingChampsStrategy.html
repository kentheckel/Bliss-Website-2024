Ring Champs — YouTube Strategy

With AK & Barak · another boxing show launched from zero

Overview

Ring Champs was another boxing show we launched from zero. Early on we helped them achieve a pretty viral moment by taking the interviews they were doing and optimizing them.

What We Did

  • Interview optimization — their interviews were the asset; we packaged them to travel.
  • A dedicated niche audience — they attract a very specific, very dedicated type of boxing fan. Even in a smaller community we found great success: longevity, real ability for brands to engage, and a strong monetization structure.
  • Rinse and repeat — we ran our main Antisocial Friends Club podcast strategy: consistent episodes, high-impact clips, title & thumbnail testing, and heavy vertical output across platforms.

Results

4.1K → 47.7K subscribers · 32.9M views since we teamed up (Mar 2025)

From a standing start to 2M+ views a month, with a community built for brand partnerships and long-term monetization.
